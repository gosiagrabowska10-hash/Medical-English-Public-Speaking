# Pronunciation Verification Log

All IPA transcriptions and stress patterns published by the Academy are checked against at least two standard British English dictionaries before publication. Transcriptions follow the dictionaries' British (UK) entries.

**Sources** (consulted on 26 November 2026)

- Cambridge Dictionary, British English pronunciation: `https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/<term>`
- Oxford Learner's Dictionaries, BrE pronunciation: `https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/<term>`
- Collins English Dictionary was also attempted, but its pages could not be read automatically because of an anti-bot check. It was not used as a source.
