**The practical skill each lesson builds**

- **L04:** choosing intelligibility rather than a native-like accent as the goal; ranking pronunciation features by how much they affect understanding (functional load); three priorities for presenters (word endings, word stress, phrase stress); selective clear speech; using the first minute to let listeners adapt; and a personal risk list built from a colleague or an automatic transcript.
- **L05:** stress patterns of common medical suffixes (-ology, -ectomy, -itis, -osis, -ic); word families in which the stress moves (metastasis / metastatic, anaesthetist / anaesthetic, analysis / analyse); stress in noun compounds; strong and weak syllables; and a personal terminology list practised by back-chaining.
- **L06:** the difference between written and spoken style; shorter sentences and known-to-new information order; verbs instead of nominalisations and the active voice; higher-level signposting instead of fillers; repetition, acronyms and contractions; and converting an abstract into a one-minute spoken script.

**How the lessons fit with what you have approved**

**Points I would particularly like your view on**

1. **Accent framing (L04).** The lesson tells learners that a native-like accent is neither necessary nor the goal, and that their accent is "part of their professional identity". Is this the message you want the Academy to give, including to learners who arrive wanting accent reduction?
2. **Automatic transcripts (L04).** The lesson suggests the automatic captions of a phone or video platform as a "second listener", always confirmed by a human. This is an external tool chosen by the learner, not part of the platform. Are you comfortable with this recommendation?
3. **Weak forms (L05).** L05 presents weak forms as natural but not essential for being understood, following the English-as-a-lingua-franca research. Would you prefer more emphasis on weak forms for advanced learners?
4. **Dictionary variation (L05).** Is presenting *diagnose* as "both forms acceptable" the right approach, or would you prefer the Academy to recommend one form?
5. **Contractions (L06).** L06 says contractions are normal in formal presentations. Some medical schools teach the opposite. Is this the right balance?
