## 2. Target audience and professional level

- **Primary audience:** consultants and specialists, clinical academics, researchers, medical educators and healthcare leaders who present at congresses, grand rounds and institutional meetings.
- **Also suitable for:** residents and early-career physicians preparing their first international presentations.
- **English level:** upper-intermediate or above (around CEFR B2+), as stated in the admissions FAQ. The workshop develops delivery skills, not general English.
- **Prior learning:** L01–L09 completed (L08 and L09 in their final versions).

**Differentiation without simplification.** Everyone works on the same competencies, using their own material:

- *Stretch options* for experienced speakers: a 5-minute version; two pressure cards instead of one; a hostile interruption card; delivering one segment without slides.
- *Support options* for early-career participants: an Academy-provided practice passage (a fictional results slide) if they have no talk of their own yet; a lower starting target for fillers.

## 3. Learning objectives and competencies

**By the end of the workshop, participants will be able to:**

1. Deliver a 2–3-minute conference segment (opening, key result, close) at a controlled pace, with deliberate pauses before and after the key result.
2. Place sentence stress and contrastive stress correctly, and stress their own ten most-used technical terms correctly.
3. Deliver critical numbers so that they cannot be misheard (show, slow down, restate).
4. Use breath, volume and pitch so that they are audible and sound authoritative, with falling intonation on statements.
5. Maintain a stable, open presence (stance, gaze, purposeful gesture) and recover within five seconds from a disruption, using a prepared phrase.
6. Measure their own delivery (words per minute, filler count, pause placement) and set an evidence-based plan for their next real presentation.

**Competencies** (the same framework will be used in all six workshops and in the final assessment rubric):

| Competency | Assessed in W1 | Programme rubric criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Vocal control (pace, pause, stress, projection) | Yes, primary focus | Voice and delivery (15%) |
| Intelligibility of key information | Yes | English fluency and intelligibility (15%) |
| Physical presence | Yes | Body language (10%) |
| Composure under pressure | Yes | Professional communication (5%), handling questions (10%) |
| Structure | Opening and close only | Structure and clarity (20%) |

## 4–5. Complete sequence and duration

The workshop runs in two equivalent modes:

- **Self-directed (default, on the platform).** The participant works alone, with a recording device. From Stage 4, the Speaking Studio and the AI Voice Coach replace manual measurement.
- **Facilitated (optional, for cohorts or institutional groups).** Groups of three rotate the roles of speaker, timekeeper and observer. A facilitator guide is included for each component.

| # | Component | Duration | What happens | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Readiness check** (before starting) | 10 min | 5 questions, 3 of them scenario-based, on L07–L09; pass mark 70%; the workshop unlocks when passed | Unlocks the workshop |
| 1 | **Briefing** | 10 min | Purpose, the six-criterion rubric, choice of material: the participant's own opening (from L02) and a key-result slide (from L07). Instructor briefing video | Chosen 2–3-minute passage |
| 2 | **Round 1 — Baseline recording** | 15 min | Record the passage as you would deliver it today. Measure words per minute on the key-result segment, count fillers, mark where you paused. Self-rate against the rubric | Recording 1 + logged measurements |
| 3 | **Station A — Pace, pause and stress clinic** | 20 min | (a) Mark thought groups and pauses on the script (L07 method). (b) "Look, pause, conclude" drill on the key data slide. (c) **Term-stress clinic:** the participant's own ten most-used terms, checked against a reference card with IPA (see §7). (d) Number drills: thirteen/thirty, fifteen/fifty, decimals and percentages | Marked script, term card |
| 4 | **Round 2 — Re-record and compare** | 10 min | Re-record the same passage following the marked script. Compare the measurements with Round 1 | Recording 2 + comparison |
| 5 | **Station B — Projection and vocal variety** (L08) | 15 min | Breath support for volume without strain; projecting to the back row and to a microphone; falling intonation on statements (avoiding rising "question" endings); pitch movement on key words; the same sentence delivered three ways | Voice warm-up routine (2 min) |
| 6 | **Station C — Presence and recovery** (L09) | 15 min | Stance and the first five seconds; gaze patterns (sections of the room, or the lens on video calls); anchoring gestures (counting on fingers, showing contrast); clicker and lectern handling; rehearsal of three recovery phrases | Personal presence checklist |
| 7 | **Round 3 — Performance under pressure** | 15 min | Deliver the passage with one randomly drawn **pressure card**: time cut from 3 to 2 minutes; slides fail; an interruption question; a microphone problem; a chair's "one minute" sign. Record and measure | Recording 3 (the final task performance) |
| 8 | **Debrief and transfer plan** | 10 min | Compare the three recordings; identify the largest gain and the main remaining habit; write the plan for the next real presentation | Transfer plan → final task |
| | **Total** | **120 min** | | |

**Facilitator guidance** will be provided for every component: purpose, set-up, timing cues, what to observe, common difficulties and how to respond, and how to give feedback using the phrase bank (§7). Example of the guidance for Round 3: *"Draw the card only after the speaker has started. Observe the first five seconds after the disruption. Do not rescue the speaker; note which recovery phrase was used and whether pace returned to target."*

## 8. Final practical task and assessment criteria

**Task.** Submit **Recording 3** (2–3 minutes: opening, key result and close, delivered under a pressure card), together with a **written analysis of 250–400 words**. The analysis compares the measurements from Rounds 1–3, identifies the largest gain and the main remaining habit, and contains a transfer plan for your next real presentation (§9).

**Assessed by:** self-assessment now. From Stage 4, AI preliminary measurement of pace, pauses and fillers, *measured metrics only*. From Stage 5, instructor review. As everywhere in the Academy, AI does not make the final judgement.

| # | Criterion | Observable evidence | Developing | Competent | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Opening** | Recording 0:00–0:40 | Apology or no stated purpose | Context, purpose and route within 40 s; ≥2 deliberate pauses | As Competent, delivered without notes and with eye contact |
| 2 | **Pace control** | Measured words per minute on the key-result segment | Above 170 wpm, or accelerating under pressure | 120–160 wpm, or ≥15% slower than Round 1 | Within range *and* pace held after the pressure card |
| 3 | **Pausing and stress** | Pause placement; stressed words | Pauses inside phrases; stress scattered | ≥1-second pause before and after the key result; one main stress per thought group | Contrastive stress used deliberately where meaning requires it |
| 4 | **Numbers and terms** | Every critical number and target term | A critical number at risk of being misheard | All critical numbers shown, slowed or restated; target terms correctly stressed | Numbers also translated into a concrete form ("one patient in seven") |
| 5 | **Voice and presence** | Audibility, intonation, stance, gaze | Fading volume; rising endings; reading the notes | Consistently audible; falling intonation on statements; looks at the audience or lens most of the time | Purposeful gestures support the structure; calm stance throughout |
| 6 | **Recovery and analysis** | Response to the pressure card; written analysis | No recovery strategy; analysis general | Recovers within 5 s using a prepared phrase; analysis uses measured values | Recovery is invisible to the audience; analysis identifies the cause of the remaining habit and a specific fix |

**Completion:** Competent or above on at least five of the six criteria, including criteria 2 and 4, which are the core intelligibility skills. Participants who do not reach this level repeat Round 3 with a new pressure card; there is no limit on attempts. The pace range is labelled as an Academy recommendation, not a research threshold.

## 9. Transfer to the next real presentation

The workshop ends with a **transfer plan** written for a specific upcoming event (a congress talk, grand round, MDT case or teaching session). It contains:

1. **The event:** date, audience, length and room or format.
2. **Three commitments**, taken from the participant's own measurements. Example: *"Key-result slide: pause 2 seconds before and after; say 'fifteen per cent, roughly one patient in seven'; keep below 150 wpm."*
3. **A two-minute voice and presence warm-up** (from Stations B and C), to be done on the day.
4. **One prepared recovery phrase** for the disruption the participant finds most likely.
5. **A one-page "speaker card"**, generated from the plan, to print or keep on the phone.

After the real event, the participant records a one-line outcome. This feeds the L03 goal tracking and is reviewed in W6 (group coaching).

## 10. Proposed template for Workshops II–VI

If this structure is approved, every workshop will follow the same pattern:

- readiness check, then briefing,
- baseline round,
- 2–3 skill stations,
- re-recording and comparison,
- a performance under realistic pressure,
- debrief and transfer plan,
- final task assessed with six observable criteria at three performance levels,
- a workshop phrase bank and a workshop pack.

The pressure element will be adapted to each workshop:

| Workshop | Pressure element | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| W2 Structure Clinic | Restructuring under a time cut | Self-directed or facilitated |
| W3 Conference Simulation | Moderated Q&A | Self-directed or facilitated |
| W4 Unexpected Questions | Escalating question difficulty | Self-directed or facilitated |
| W5 Media Interview | Bridging under a journalist's pressure | Self-directed or facilitated |
| W6 Group Coaching and Dress Rehearsal | Live audience | Live, with a human instructor |
