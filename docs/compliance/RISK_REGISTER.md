# Project Risk Register

**Maintained from:** 27 November 2026 · **Review:** at every milestone and before launch · **Companion:** `COMPLIANCE_REGISTER.md` (requirements) and `CHANGES.md` (evidence of completed actions)

> **Status statement.** The Academy platform has implemented and tested a number of technical controls, but it is **not** described as fully GDPR-compliant, legally certified or production-ready. That description requires the independent checks listed below to be completed: specialist legal and data-protection review, an independent accessibility audit, penetration testing, medical and language review of content, and a production hosting configuration.

Scale: Likelihood and Impact — Low / Medium / High. Status — Open / Mitigating / Closed.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Current controls | Action required | Trigger / deadline | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **CSP still allows inline scripts** (`'unsafe-inline'`), weakening protection against cross-site scripting | Medium | High | Full header set; React output escaping; no user-generated HTML rendered; dependency auditing | Move to a nonce- or hash-based CSP (requires dynamic rendering or experimental SRI); test all pages | Before public launch | Technical lead | Open |
| R2 | **Staff access not restricted to assigned groups** — any instructor with MFA can read every learner's progress and submissions | Medium | High | MFA required for all staff privileges; role grants audited; staff cannot assess themselves | Cohort-assignment table; restrict staff RLS policies to assigned cohorts; tests | Before the first real cohort | Technical lead / Academic Director | Open |
| R3 | **Production backups and alerting depend on the hosted Supabase configuration**, which is not connected | High (until configured) | High | Tested local backup-and-restore script; documented procedure; error monitoring and health endpoint in code | Create EU-region project; enable daily backups and PITR; uptime monitor and alert routing; first production restore test | Before any real learner data | Academy Owner / technical lead | Open |
| R4 | **Manual screen-reader audit outstanding** — automated checks find only part of WCAG issues | Medium | Medium | axe-core gate (0 violations, 38 views + staff/MFA views); keyboard and focus design; tagged PDFs | Independent manual audit (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, zoom, speech input); PDF/UA validation; update Accessibility Statement | Before public launch | Academy Owner | Open |
| R5 | **Medical and language review of educational content required** before publication | Medium | High | Content standard; references cited; fictional cases; pronunciation verified against dictionaries; automated content quality gate | Medical educator and English-language specialist sign-off per lesson; second-person reference verification; review log | Before any content is published to paying learners | Academic Director | Open |
| R6 | **Legal policies require external specialist review** (Terms, Privacy, Cookies, withdrawal, complaints, RoPA, DPIA, retention, processor agreements, AI Act classification) | High (until reviewed) | High | Drafts clearly labelled; register separates technical controls from legal requirements; validator blocks “Compliant” for unreviewed legal items | Engage a Polish/EU consumer and e-commerce lawyer, a data-protection specialist, an AI-regulation specialist and a tax adviser | Before accepting learners or payments | Academy Owner | Open |
