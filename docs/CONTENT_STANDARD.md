# Academy Content Standard

Approved by the Academy on 2026-11-25. Applies to every lesson and workshop of the Medical Public Speaking Professional Programme. Implemented in `src/content/lesson-types.ts`; reference lessons: L01, L02, L03, L07 (pending final approval).

## Main lessons (30)
| Component | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Learning objectives | 3–4, observable ("deliver", "rewrite", "answer"), not "understand" |
| Teaching content | **1,200–1,800 words** of substantive content: introduction, 4–7 sections, evidence/recommendation notes and examples. Quiz, phrase bank, headings and instructions are not counted |
| Professional examples | At least 3, detailed, from realistic settings (congress, grand round, MDT, journal club, teaching, media) across specialties; composite and fictional; no patient-identifiable information |
| Phrase bank | 8–12 expressions, each with use, register, pronunciation guidance where relevant and an example in a real presentation, plus guidance on using them in the student's own talks |
| Demonstration brief | What the instructor video shows and what to notice |
| Common mistakes | 4–6, each with the correction |
| Guided exercise | Step-by-step instructions, a fictional model answer (revealed after the student saves) and self-check criteria |
| Knowledge check | 5–6 questions, at least half scenario-based, with an explanation for every answer; pass mark 70% |
| Assignment | Applied to the student's own work, with transparent assessment criteria |
| Reflection | 2–3 questions |
| Materials | Lesson handout PDF generated from the lesson (`npm run content:handouts`) |
| Completion | Exercise saved, quiz passed, assignment submitted (enforced by the database) |
| Duration | 50–65 minutes |

## Practical workshops (6)
A separate practice-oriented structure: briefing, realistic scenarios, timed practice rounds, detailed instructions, facilitator guidance, self/peer assessment and assessment criteria. W6 is the live group coaching session. The structure of W1 will be presented for approval before all six are written.

## Evidence and accuracy
- No invented research findings, statistics, testimonials, endorsements, accreditation or CME/CPD claims.
- Three visibly labelled kinds of statement: **Research evidence** (summarises cited, real, verifiable publications), **Professional recommendation** (Academy guidance, not a research finding), **Illustrative example** (composite, fictional).
- Specific research-based claims carry numbered references; references must be checked against the original by the Academic Director before launch.
- Where evidence is nuanced or contested, say so.
- No clinical advice; medical examples illustrate communication only.

## Language
- British English throughout ("Programme", "Simulation Centre", recognise, practise/practice, anaesthetist, paediatric); internationally understandable medical and professional English.
- Spoken-style English in examples and phrase banks; clear written style in teaching text.
